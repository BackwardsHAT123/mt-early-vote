Methods and caveats

Source. Montana Secretary of State, 2026 General Election Absentee Ballot Counts (county election officials report; the state says it updates daily).

Counting. "Received" is absentee ballots returned to county offices, not necessarily accepted or counted. Rates divide by ballots sent, not registered voters.

Lincoln County shows 0 received in this snapshot, which most likely means it has not reported yet rather than that no ballots came back. It is included in the totals as published.

Totals. The dashboard's "sent" total () is lower than the 575,000+ the Secretary of State cited in an October 9 release, so the two may be counted at different times.

2024 comparison. Statewide only. The state does not publish daily 2024 county counts, so the dashed line connects four published statewide figures for the 2024 general (Nov 5): about 125,000 received at 14 days out and about 285,000 at 8 days (both from Secretary of State releases, dates inferred from "two weeks" and "one week"), 312,814 on Oct 29 (7 days, Daily Montanan), 338,832 on Oct 30 (6 days) and 361,865 on Oct 31 (5 days) (both from Secretary of State counts reported by NBC Montana), and about 425,000 as of 5 p.m. Nov 4 (1 day). Values between those points are straight-line interpolations, so treat the same-day comparison as approximate.

2022. The only 2022 figure found is about 284,000 absentee ballots received the day before Election Day (Nov 7, 2022), as cited by Daily Montanan and other news outlets. No daily 2022 series was found, so it appears as a single marker rather than a line.

History. Only this one snapshot is embedded. Add a new entry to SNAPSHOTS in the page source each day to build a cumulative curve and compare against 2022 or 2024 once you have those counts.
